# Supported Sites (M0)

No site is yet declared supported. The extractor prototype only considers visible HTML `<img>` elements with HTTP(S) `currentSrc`, natural dimensions of at least 200×200, and rendered dimensions of at least 100×100. These provisional filters must be replaced or confirmed by the M0 physical-device measurements.

Canvas readers, protected media, authenticated content capture, cookies, sessions, and DRM bypass are out of scope.
